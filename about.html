Our story

Started because handovers kept failing

Novix began in 2019 after one too many projects arrived at our door half-finished: good ideas, decent code, and nobody left who understood how it ran.

So we built the studio around the opposite habit. Documentation written as the feature lands. Your engineers in our code reviews from week one. Infrastructure defined in files you own rather than clicks nobody recorded. Six years and 120 projects later, that is still the whole pitch — we leave you able to run it without us.

Principles

How we decide things

Six rules that settle most arguments before they start.

Say the hard thing early

If a deadline is unrealistic or a feature is not worth building, you hear it in week one — not in the retrospective.

Own the outcome

We are not finished when the ticket closes. We are finished when the thing works in production for real users.

Teach as we build

Your engineers sit in our reviews. By handover, nothing about the codebase is a mystery to your team.

Craft over volume

We take on fewer projects than we could, so the ones we take get senior attention from start to finish.

Small, reversible steps

Short-lived branches, feature flags and staged rollouts. Every change has a way back.

Treat people well

Sustainable hours, honest invoices and no surprises. It is the only way to keep a team worth hiring.
