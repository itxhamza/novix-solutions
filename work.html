How a typical engagement runs

Most of our projects follow the same shape: a short discovery to remove unknowns, a thin vertical slice to prove the architecture, then steady sprints until launch.

  • Week 1–2. Discovery, success metrics and a clickable prototype.
  • Week 3–4. A working vertical slice in staging, deployed by CI.
  • Week 5+. Feature sprints with weekly demos and a visible burn-up.
  • Launch. Staged rollout behind flags, monitored, with a rollback path.
