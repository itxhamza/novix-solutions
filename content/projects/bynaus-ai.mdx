Bynaus is an AI workspace for construction operations, made for general
contractors, specialty subcontractors and the suppliers around them. It takes
the place of the usual stack — a project management tool, a timesheet app and a
folder of compliance PDFs — with one workspace the office and the field both
work in.

What makes it more than a tracker is BynausAI. Ask it the prevailing wage on a
job and it does not answer from memory: it retrieves the official determination,
archives a screenshot of the source, hashes that evidence with SHA-256, and
returns the rate with the compliance notes and every link it used. The answer is
built to survive an audit rather than to sound right. Ask it for a board and it
builds one against the project you pick.

Around the assistant sits the operational surface. Projects are created through a
six-step setup covering cost codes, alerts, contacts and time settings. Hours
come in through daily and weekly timesheets and a kiosk the crew clocks in and
out of. Change orders, people, and HR and safety issues are tracked in the same
place, and a Teams meeting is one click from the dashboard.

Novix built all of it: the workspace, the assistant, and the backend they both
run on.
