Runout is a Texas Hold'em trainer built around the decisions that actually move
a win rate. Rather than playing out thousands of hands to meet a spot once, you
drill the spot directly: opening ranges from every position, 3-bet and defence,
and the postflop choice between betting, checking, bluffing and value.

Each day's session is generated against how you have been playing, so the drills
follow your weak spots rather than a fixed syllabus. Every decision comes back
with an explanation — the reasoning behind the play, not a right-or-wrong mark —
and the range, pot odds and equity work behind it runs across stack depths and
positions.

It runs on Android and on the web, and it runs offline, with a practice mode
that shuffles new scenarios indefinitely. None of it is real-money play: it is a
training tool.

Novix built all of it, on Android and the web.
