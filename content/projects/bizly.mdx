Bizly is a network built for creative work, where the profile is the portfolio.
Filmmakers, performers, designers and other professionals put their work, their
résumé and their contact details on a single page anyone can open — a personal
site that happens to live inside a network.

The other side of it is hiring. Clients and brands look for talent through an
AI-powered search rather than casting a wide net and hoping, and creatives meet
them halfway: opportunities are posted and applied to in the app, and peers can
be referred into them.

Novix built all of it, on iOS and Android.
