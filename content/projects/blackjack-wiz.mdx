Blackjack Wiz is a trainer rather than a game. It teaches basic strategy and
the Hi-Lo counting system through drills and corrects every hand as you play.
Everything is simulated: no real money goes in, and nothing can be won.

A session deals through a full shoe. You keep the running count, convert it to
the true count, and the app tells you the moment a decision or a count goes
wrong — the part a strategy chart on a phone screen cannot do. Speed drills put
a clock on counting down a shoe, and the deviation charts cover when to leave
basic strategy behind.

The table rules are yours to set: whether the dealer hits or stands on soft 17,
double after split, surrender, resplitting aces, the number of decks and the
penetration. A built-in house edge calculator shows what each of those choices
costs. All of it runs offline.

Novix built all of it, on iOS and Android.
