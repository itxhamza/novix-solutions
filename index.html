Questions

Answers before you ask

How quickly can we get started?

Most engagements kick off within one to two weeks. We start with a paid discovery sprint so the first build sprint begins with a validated scope rather than assumptions.

Do you work with our existing team?

Often, yes. We can run an embedded squad alongside your engineers, take full ownership of a workstream, or do both across different phases of the project.

How do you price projects?

Fixed-scope work is quoted after discovery. Longer programmes run on a monthly retainer for a dedicated squad. Either way you get the full rate card before you commit.

Who owns the code and the IP?

You do, from the first commit. Everything lives in your repositories and cloud accounts, and we hand over documentation and a walkthrough at the end of every engagement.

What happens after launch?

We offer support retainers covering monitoring, security patching, performance budgets and a roadmap of iterative improvements — or a clean handover if you would rather run it in-house.
