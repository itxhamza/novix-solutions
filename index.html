What it is like to work with us

Questions

Answers before you ask

How quickly can we start?

Usually one to two weeks. The first thing we do is a paid discovery week, which mostly means asking awkward questions until the scope stops moving. It is the cheapest week of the project and it saves the most.

Can you work alongside our own engineers?

Yes, and it is normally the better arrangement. Your developers review our pull requests from the first week. Where your team is stretched we take a whole workstream off them instead. Most projects end up doing both at different stages.

How do you price work?

Fixed scope gets a fixed number, quoted once discovery is done and the unknowns are actually known. Open-ended work runs monthly. You see the rate card before you commit to anything, and the monthly arrangement is cancellable on 30 days’ notice.

Who owns the code?

You do, from the first commit. It lives in your repository and your cloud account, not ours. That is also why we can say the apps on our work page would keep running if we disappeared — the keys were never ours to begin with.

What happens after launch?

Whatever suits you. Some clients keep us on for monitoring, store releases and the next round of features. Others take it in house and we hear from them once a year. We build it the same way either way, so neither decision costs you anything.

What if we are not sure what we need yet?

Then say that. A paragraph describing the problem is enough to start. We will tell you what we think it is, roughly what it costs, and whether you should be spending the money yet — including the times the answer is no.
